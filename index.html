Foods to Eliminate

  • Liquid Sugars: Soda, juice, sweetened drinks.
  • "Beige" Carbs: White bread, white pasta, white rice.
  • "Fake" Health Foods: Coconut oil, granola, veggie chips.

Trans Fat Warning

Always read the ingredient list. Manufacturers can label a product as "0g Trans Fat" if it has < 0.5g per serving.

Look for: "Hydrogenated" or "Partially Hydrogenated" oils.

Smart Cooking & Preparation

How you prepare food is just as important as what you eat. Use these techniques to maximize nutrient absorption and improve texture.

Cooking Hacks (Click to Expand)

Freeze a block of firm tofu, then thaw it. Squeeze out all the water (it will come out easily). The ice crystals create a honeycomb structure, making the tofu chewy and incredibly absorbent, like a sponge for sauces.

Bake salmon in a sealed parchment paper packet at 400°F (200°C) for 12–14 minutes. This steam-baking method preserves delicate Omega-3 fats and prevents the oil oxidation that occurs during high-heat pan-frying.

To reduce gas from canned beans, rinse them in a colander for 30 seconds until the bubbles stop. To thicken soups or pasta sauces without cream, mash half the beans into a paste before adding them to the dish.

Raw vs. Cooked: Nutrient Guide

Cooking breaks down tough cell walls, making nutrients easier to absorb.

  • Spinach: Cooking reduces oxalates, making Iron and Calcium far more absorbable.
  • Tomatoes: Heat releases heart-healthy Lycopene.
  • Carrots: Cooking boosts absorbable Beta-Carotene (Vitamin A).
  • Mushrooms: Breaks down hard-to-digest chitin and kills potential toxins.
  • Asparagus: Makes vitamins A and E more available.

Worst Method: Boiling (vitamins leach into water). Best Methods: Steaming or Roasting.

Heat can destroy fragile, water-soluble vitamins like Vitamin C.

  • Bell Peppers: Packed with Vitamin C, which heat destroys.
  • Broccoli: Raw broccoli contains myrosinase (a cancer-fighting enzyme) that cooking kills. (Light steaming is a good compromise).
  • Onions & Garlic: Raw garlic's allicin (boosts immunity) is destroyed by heat. Let garlic sit 10 mins after chopping *before* cooking to preserve some benefits.
  • Nuts & Seeds: Roasting can damage delicate Omega-3 fats. Eat walnuts, flax, and chia raw.

Some foods offer different benefits depending on preparation.

  • Kale (Raw): Higher in Vitamin C and antioxidants.
  • Kale (Cooked): Easier to digest, and you can eat a larger volume (getting more Vitamin K and fiber).

Pro Tip: The Iron + Vitamin C Rule

Iron from plants (like spinach and lentils) is hard for the body to absorb. You must pair these foods with a Vitamin C source to unlock the iron.

Examples: Squeeze lemon juice on lentil soup, or add raw bell peppers to a spinach salad.

7-Day Sample Meal Plan

This plan integrates all the core principles, substitutions, and cooking hacks into a practical week. Use the tabs to navigate between days.

🌅 The Non-Negotiable Daily Routine

  1. Breakfast: The "Artery Scrubber" Bowl (Oats, flax, walnuts, berries, cinnamon).
  2. Supplement: 1 tsp Psyllium Husk in a full glass of water.
  3. Activity: **15-Minute Brisk Walk** immediately after breakfast.

Day 1: The "Freezer Trick" Tofu (High Soy)

  • Lunch: Edamame & Quinoa Salad (1 cup edamame, quinoa, raw bell peppers, lemon-tahini dressing).
  • Snack: Green apple slices with hemp hearts.
  • Dinner: "Freezer Trick" Tofu Stir-Fry (Use thawed tofu, ginger, garlic, lightly steamed broccoli).

Day 2: The "Paper Bag" Omega-3 Boost

  • Lunch: Creamy Lentil Soup ("Rinse & Mash" technique; add cooked spinach & lemon juice).
  • Snack: Unsweetened Soy Milk latte + handful of Pistachios.
  • Dinner: "Paper Bag" Salmon (Baked 12 mins at 400°F). Side of roasted sweet potato and asparagus.

Day 3: The Iron Absorption Day

  • Lunch: Tempeh "BLT" Wraps (Seared tempeh, lettuce, tomato, avocado).
  • Snack: 1/2 cup Cottage Cheese with berries.
  • Dinner: Warm Spinach & Chickpea Salad (Sautéed spinach, chickpeas, nutritional yeast) with fresh orange segments on the side (for Vitamin C).

Day 4: The Anti-Inflammatory Reset

  • Lunch: Leftover Salmon (or Tofu) Cold Salad (Mixed greens, avocado, walnuts).
  • Snack: Raw carrots with hummus.
  • Dinner: Turmeric & Ginger Stew (Red lentils, turmeric, ginger; add kale at the end).

Day 5: The "Soy Strategy" Peak

  • Lunch: Soy Milk Smoothie (Soy milk, chia, berries, spinach) & Avocado Toast.
  • Snack: Handful of Almonds.
  • Dinner: Miso-Glazed Eggplant & Edamame (Side of 1 cup steamed edamame).

Day 6: The Gut Health Focus

  • Lunch: Mediterranean Quinoa Bowl (Quinoa, cucumber, olives, parsley, hemp seeds).
  • Snack: Pear (skin on) with a few walnuts.
  • Dinner: Kimchi & Tofu Stew (Tofu, Kimchi, cooked mushrooms).

Day 7: The "Clean Out" & Prep

  • Lunch: "Everything" Bean Chili ("Rinse & Mash" beans, cooked tomatoes, bell peppers).
  • Snack: Unsweetened Soy Milk + 1 tbsp Chia Seeds (mini pudding).
  • Dinner: Roasted Root Vegetable Platter (Carrots, beets, onions) drizzled with EVOO *after* cooking.
